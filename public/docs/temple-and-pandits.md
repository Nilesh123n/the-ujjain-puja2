# Sacred Temples & Vedic Scholars of Ujjain

## 1. Pardeshwar Mahadev Mandir (World's Largest Paras Shivling)
- Located near Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga, Ujjain.
- Houses the world's largest consecrated solidified Mercury (Paras/Rasalingam) Shivling.
- Described in classical Tantric texts (*Rasarnava Tantra*) as having boundless spiritual potency where worship results multiply thousand-fold.

## 2. Mangalnath Mandir (Birthplace of Mars)
- Cosmological center according to *Matsya Purana* and *Skanda Purana*.
- Premier global destination for Mangal Dosh Nivaran (Bhaat Puja).

## 3. Triveni Shani Navgrah Mandir
- Sacred confluence of Shipra, Khan, and mythical Saraswati rivers.
- Consecrated for nullifying planetary afflictions and Saturn transits.

## 4. Qualified Vedic Scholars & Pandits
- All conducting priests are accredited scholars from traditional Sanskrit Gurukuls (Kashi Vidyapeeth, Maharshi Sandipani Rashtriya Veda Vidya Pratishthan).
- Complete adherence to authentic Vedic injunctions, correct Sanskrit pronunciation, and pure samagri.
